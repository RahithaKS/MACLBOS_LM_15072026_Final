---
name: Workspace frame standards
description: Shared visual and scrolling rules for authenticated LedgerLM and standalone workspace pages.
---

Authenticated LedgerLM and standalone workspace pages use one full-height content frame: consistent gutters, a rounded surface, a stable header area, and an independently scrolling body.

**Why:** Mixing document-level scrolling, inconsistent page gutters, and different content-frame heights made pages feel like separate products and caused key actions to disappear while scrolling.

**How to apply:** Reuse the workspace frame sizing rules for new authenticated pages. Keep authentication, welcome, download, and modal layouts separate. In standalone pages, mirror the same frame dimensions while preserving the direct-use sidebar.
